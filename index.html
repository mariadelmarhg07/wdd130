Hi everyone! My name is Mar, and I was born and raised in Cali, Colombia. I'm 25 years old, happily married, and have a bachelor's degree in Biology. I played the cello for five years, and I love dancing, singing, and drawing. My biggest weakness is Reese's chocolate and red berry ice cream! I'm currently working as a Project Coordinator at Fundación OCEANIDAD, a Colombian NGO focused on marine conservation. I'm leading a shark research project in the Gulf of Tribugá, where we're monitoring sharks using environmental DNA (eDNA). I'm excited to learn with all of you and get to know everyone better!

COLOMBIA

Colombia is a beautiful and diverse country located in the northwest of South America, known for its incredible biodiversity, vibrant culture, and warm, welcoming people. It is the only South American country with coastlines on both the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea, offering a variety of landscapes, from tropical rainforests and majestic mountains to beautiful beaches and vast plains. Colombia is also famous for its coffee, music, traditional cuisine, colorful festivals, and rich cultural heritage. It is a country where nature, tradition, and joy come together, making it a truly unique place to live and visit.
